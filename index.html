Our Approach

Learning is not a preparation for life; it is life itself. Rooted in Dewey, inspired by emergent gameplay, our education is open-world and purpose-driven.

Read more about our philosophy

At the Leeuwarden School of Design, we do not use admissions criteria. Everyone with curiosity, commitment, and imagination is welcome – whether for a bachelor’s, master’s, or individual course. Through this we enable all kinds of interactions that we think enrich education.

For us, education is more than knowledge transfer. It is a shared practice, a world in itself. That's why we don't tie up our education in a strict set of rules. We base it on five pillars:

– Community-based learning: you never learn alone – learning always happens in relation to others: students, lecturers, clients, and users.

– Competency-based education: you develop the skills, attitudes, and knowledge you need to act as a designer.

– Practice-driven learning: learning and doing are inseparable. We work on real challenges in the real world.

– Cosmopolitanism: our perspective is open and international. We learn from other views, other cultures, and other worlds.

– Demand-driven learning: your motivation matters. What you want to research or develop becomes the starting point of your learning path.

Whether you follow a short course or a full master’s program, you become part of a learning community that designs the world of tomorrow.

But good education is more than just accountability.

Rooted education

Our education is firmly rooted in design principles, contemporary theory, and practical experience. Every course, bachelor’s, master’s, or PhD trajectory is part of one cohesive curriculum – built around design as a way of thinking and making.

Open World - Emergent Education

Ou education is welcoming. Where possible, we aim for minimal structure – to give students maximum space for initiative, depth, and application.

We see education as an open world. Like in emergent gameplay, meaning emerges through interaction between player and environment. Not everything is predetermined. Direction and significance arise from choices, events, and connections.

That’s also how we view development: what you do, learn, and create becomes assessable the moment you can connect it to your development as a designer. 

What you learn gains value in relation to what you do – and vice versa.

We work situationally and flexibly. That means we tailor learning paths to the level, needs, and context of each student. Expect to be guided in relation to your needs: we direct, support, coach and delegate. In contexts that range from short courses to full-time degrees, from entry-level to doctoral research: the form may change, but the underlying philosophy remains constant.

Education is not a fixed scaffold it's a modular framework. 

Education is not a prelude to the real work. It is the real work.
